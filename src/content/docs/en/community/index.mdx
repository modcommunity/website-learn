## The shape of it

Almost everything member-authored on this site can be **reported**. A report
opens a thread between the reporter and staff.

A report may or may not lead to a **penalty**. A penalty is attached either to a
**member** or to a **content item**, and what it restricts depends on its type —
they are not one ladder. A penalty may expire or may not.

Penalties on servers are a special case: most of them are raised by automated
detectors rather than by people, and each detector may only lift its own. See
[Penalties](/learn/community/penalties#server-flags) and
[Scanning](/learn/servers/scanning).
