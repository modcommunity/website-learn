Some new content is **held** until somebody approves it: a moderator, the owner of the app it is filed under, or the owner of the item it was written on. A held post is saved and visible to you, its author, but to nobody else until it is approved. When you post something that is held, the site says **Submitted for approval** rather than leaving you to wonder where it went.

## What can be held

| What | Held by |
| --- | --- |
| A new app on the TMC Gaming Platform | The site, by default. An app gets its own top-level address, so it is looked at first. |
| Assets, mods, servers, communities, collections, groups | The site, or the app the item is filed under. |
| Comments, reviews, gallery uploads, bug reports and replies, feedback, discussion posts, questions | The site, the app, or **the owner of the item they are written on** — a perk of the item owner's role (Supporter and above). |
| Releases | Game releases on apps that review them, and **any release carrying a dangerous file** (below). |

Edits to something already published are not held again, and nobody is held by their own app or item: an owner never waits for themselves, and moderators are never held.

## Who decides

A request can need one or both of two sides:

- **The moderators**, when the site's own policy holds it, when the app it belongs to has no owner, or when a file matched the site-wide dangerous list.
- **The owner** of the app or item whose policy held it. Anybody who can edit that app or item can decide for it.

A moderator's approval covers both sides, so a moderator can push anything through. An owner's approval covers only the owner's side. Either side rejecting rejects the request, and the author is told why.

## Dangerous files

Executables and other risky file types can be listed — once for the whole site, and separately per app. A release carrying one is held instead of refused, and every part of a file name counts (`setup.exe.txt` is an executable), including the files **inside** an archive once it has been indexed. A file on the site-wide list needs a moderator; a file on an app's own list needs that app's owner (or a moderator, if the app has none).

## Where you see it

- **Your approvals** (`/approvals`): what is waiting for you to review, what you submitted and where it stands, and your settings — which kinds of content your apps and your items hold.
- **Moderation → Approvals**: the moderators' queue and the site-wide policy, beside the other [moderation](/learn/community/moderation) queues.
- A held item's own page shows a banner with **Approve** and **Reject** to whoever can decide it, and held comments and reviews are marked **Not public** for their author.

Every decision is in the moderation log, and the author gets a notification either way.
