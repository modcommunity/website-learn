The **TMC app** is the site's desktop client. It does what the website cannot
do from inside a browser: put files into a game's folder, measure latency to a
server from *your* machine, and start a game as a real program.

## Installing

Downloads are on the [app's page](/tmc-app) and on the
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/modcommunity/tmc-app/releases).

- **Linux** — one line, no root:
  `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/modcommunity/tmc-app/main/scripts/install.sh | sh`.
  It checks the download against the release's checksums, installs into
  `~/.local`, and registers the launcher entry and the `tmc://` link scheme.
  `… | sh -s -- --uninstall` removes it. AppImage and `.deb` builds are also
  published.
- **Windows** — the `setup.exe` or `.msi` installer. The portable `.exe` runs
  without installing, and so does **not** register the `tmc://` scheme: links
  from the website will not open it.
- **macOS** — the `.dmg`.

## Signing in

The app signs in with a **device code**: it shows a short code, you approve it
on the website while signed in, and the app receives its own token. It never
sees your password, and you can revoke it under **Account → Sessions** — see [Keys and integrations](/learn/account/credentials) and, for the
protocol, [App API authentication](/learn/api/app/auth).

## What it does

- **Mods.** One-click install and uninstall for games that support it, and
  **sandboxes** — named mod profiles per game, each with its own load order and
  deployment method. Switching sandboxes does not re-download anything.
- **Servers.** A server browser with **real-time latency** measured from your
  machine, status and latency charts, RCON with history, and one-click connect
  where the game supports it.
- **Games.** Every game has its own page, and TMC's own games are installed,
  updated and launched by the app — see [Games and playing](/learn/app/games).
- **Friends and parties.** The same friends and parties the website and the
  games themselves show — see [Friends and parties](/learn/app/friends).

<Callout type="info" title="The app and the website are one account">
Subscriptions, friends, parties and statistics live on the site, not in the
app. Anything you do in one shows up in the other, and uninstalling the app
loses nothing but its local files and sandboxes.
</Callout>
