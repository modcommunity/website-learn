The app's **Friends** page is the same friends and parties you have on the
website and inside the games that support them — there is one friend list and
one party per account, and all three show it.

## Your friends

Friends are sorted by what they are doing: **in a game**, **online**,
**away**, then **offline**. For a friend in a game the row names the game and,
where they are on a listed server, the server:

- **Join** appears when their game has said they can be joined — it opens
  their server's page, and **Play** there launches you onto it;
- **Server** appears when they are on a server but have not said they are
  joinable — it opens the server's page instead.

**Remove friend** is on each row.

<Callout type="info" title="Offline is sometimes a privacy setting">
A friend whose [privacy settings](/learn/account/privacy) hide their activity
reads as offline to everybody, friends included, and a friend who is only on the
website shows as online or away rather than in a game. Presence a game reports
also expires on its own a short while after the game stops sending it, so a
crash shows as offline rather than as "in game" forever.
</Callout>

## Requests

- **Add friend** is on every member's page in the app.
- **Requests** lists what you have been sent — **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **Sent** lists what you have asked — **Withdraw** takes a request back.

The rules are the website's: asking somebody who has already asked you accepts
them; after a decline only the person declined waits before asking again; and
a member can turn friend requests off entirely.

## Party invites and your party

**Party invites** lists invitations to a [party](/learn/parties) with
**Join** and **Decline**. Accepting still goes through the party's own rules,
so a party that has filled since you were invited turns you away and the invite
stays usable.

**Your party** shows the party you are in, if any, with its roster and
**Leave**. Being in a party from the app is the same as being in it from the
website or from a game — see [Joining and visibility](/learn/parties/joining)
and [Lifecycle](/learn/parties/lifecycle).
