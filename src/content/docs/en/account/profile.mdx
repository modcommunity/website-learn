## Two names

<Params>
  <Param name="username">
    The stable handle. It appears in your profile URL and is what people type
    to find you. Case-insensitively unique — `Ashley` and `ashley` cannot be two
    accounts.

    A brand-new account is given a generated one (`modder_1a2b3c4d`) rather than
    being left without, so there is never a state where your profile cannot be
    linked to.
  </Param>
  <Param name="name">
    The display name — what people read. Also unique, case-insensitively.
  </Param>
</Params>

Your profile lives at `/u/<id>-<username>`. The id is what makes the link
survive a rename.

## Avatar

Uploaded like any other file, and owned by you. Removing it falls back to a
generated placeholder rather than a blank.

## What your profile shows

Each of these is a switch, on by default, under **Account → Privacy**:

| Section | Switch |
| --- | --- |
| The profile at all | `showProfile` |
| Your activity feed | `showActivity` |
| Your friends | `showFriends` |
| Your followers | `showFollowers` |
| Your status updates | `showStatusUpdates` |
| Your media | `showMedia` |
| Your game history | `showGameHistory` |
| You in the member browser | `showPublic` |

They are explained in full on [Privacy and
anonymity](/learn/account/privacy), including the two stronger switches that
override all of them.

## Your own sections

A profile carries more than a bio:

- **Articles** — anything you have published under your own name
- **Content** — your mods, assets, servers, collections and groups
- **Activity** — what you have been doing, if you show it
- **Friends** and **Followers**
- **Media** — your gallery contributions
- **Status updates** — short posts, which take comments and ratings of their own
