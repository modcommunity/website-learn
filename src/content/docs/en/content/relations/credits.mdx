A **contributor** is someone who helped make an item. They appear on the item's
Contributors tab and in a short list in the sidebar.

## A contributor does not need an account

This is the point of the model. A contributor row carries **either** a linked
account **or** a plain name — at least one, and it may have both:

<Params>
  <Param name="userId" type="string">
    The platform account, when the person has one. Linking it means a later
    rename follows automatically, and the credit links to their profile.
  </Param>
  <Param name="name" type="string">
    A plain credit for somebody off-platform — a Discord handle, a studio, a
    person who never signed up. Ignored for display when `userId` is set: the
    linked account's name wins.
  </Param>
  <Param name="role" type="string" required>
    What they did on the project — `Developer`, `Artist`, `Translator`.
  </Param>
  <Param name="contributionType" type="string" required>
    What kind of work it was — `Code`, `Art`, `Design`, `Testing`.

    Kept free-form rather than an enum on purpose: every project credits
    differently, and an enum would need a schema change per community
    convention.
  </Param>
  <Param name="contributionDescription" type="markdown">
    The detail, where the role and type do not cover it.
  </Param>
  <Param name="contributionUrl" type="url">
    A link to the work — a pull request, a portfolio piece.
  </Param>
  <Param name="contributionDate" type="datetime">
    When the work happened.
  </Param>
  <Param name="weight" type="int" default="0">
    Ordering. Higher sorts first, which is what the sidebar's short list reads;
    ties fall back to when the credit was added.
  </Param>
</Params>

## Credit is not access

Crediting somebody does **not** give them any ability to edit the item. That is
a [permission grant](/learn/content/relations/permissions), which is a separate
thing with a separate tab. A credit is a statement about the past; a grant is a
capability in the present.

## Notifications

Members notified about credits (on by default, under **Account →
Notifications**) hear about it when they are credited on something.
